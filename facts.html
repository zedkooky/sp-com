Siddhartha Parmar: facts and biography

Siddhartha Parmar (known as Sid) is a Zambian founder based in Lusaka. He builds companies across space technology, renewable computing, agricultural intelligence, mining and copper trading, and music in Zambia and Sub-Saharan Africa. This page is the short, factual reference for his work.

Companies

What Siddhartha Parmar has built.

Each entry states the relationship plainly.

SpaceZ
Siddhartha Parmar is the founder of SpaceZ (formerly IgnitoSPACE), described as Zambia’s first space company. SpaceZ turns satellite imagery and analysis into decisions about crops, water, land use and climate risk. It won the SANSA Africa Earth Observation Challenge and the NewSpace Africa Pitch Competition.
Quantum Hash Ventures
Siddhartha Parmar is a co-founder of Quantum Hash Ventures, which places modular data centres next to renewable generation so that surplus power from rural mini-grids can be sold. Its first deployment is at the Zengamina hydro site.
AgriNova
Siddhartha Parmar builds AgriNova, which turns satellite data and AI into field-level advisory for smallholder farmers. It is in pilot with the Cotton Board of Zambia, covering about 30,000 farmers.
Zambian Outdoor Adventure Company
Siddhartha Parmar builds the Zambian Outdoor Adventure Company (ZOAC) with Hennie Smit. It offers commercial and rescue diving, including mine rescue dive training and equipment supply for the Copperbelt.
Nyimboo
Siddhartha Parmar builds Nyimboo, a Zambian music streaming platform designed around local payment methods, data costs and rights.
Aurum Resources
Siddhartha Parmar founded Aurum Resources, a trading and advisory desk for copper cathode and concentrate, mine sale and acquisition mandates, and procurement for outside investors. See Aurum Resources.
Property
Siddhartha Parmar works in property development and sales, including properties for lease and sale, mandates to sell on behalf of clients, and low-profile storage units on the Mukatasha property for CBD shop owners in Lusaka.
