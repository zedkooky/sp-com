About

Who is Siddhartha Parmar?

Siddhartha Parmar (Sid) is a Zambian founder based in Lusaka. He builds companies across space technology, renewable computing, agricultural intelligence, mining and copper trading, and music. He is the founder of SpaceZ (formerly IgnitoSPACE), described as Zambia’s first space company, a co-founder of Quantum Hash Ventures, and the founder of Aurum Resources. He works with investors, operators and partners entering and building in Zambia and Sub-Saharan Africa.

Why I build

Africa does not need a copy of Silicon Valley.

It needs infrastructure designed for the conditions that actually exist here. Intermittent power. Missing maps. Thin capital. Long distances. Those are not excuses. They are the design brief.

Satellites where there are no maps.

Hydro that earns instead of waiting for customers.

Advisory that reaches a feature phone.

Rescue divers trained here, not flown in.

Artists paid in the country they record in.

Every venture has to survive without charity. If it cannot, it is a project, not a business.
